Cookie Policy

This website uses a very small number of cookies / browser local-storage entries — far less than most sites. Here's exactly what's used and why.

What we don't use

We don't run advertising cookies, third-party tracking/analytics cookies, or cross-site tracking of any kind on this website. The fonts and front-end framework (Bootstrap, Google Fonts) are loaded from their providers' content delivery networks to make the site load faster, and those providers may log basic technical request data (like your IP address) as part of normal web hosting — this is standard for any website using a CDN and isn't something we control or receive.

The software itself

OKV IMS LAN Edition (the desktop/LAN software) does not use browser cookies in the traditional sense — it's a local web application. It uses a login session token stored in your browser's local storage on each device, purely so you stay logged in on that device. This never leaves your local network.

Managing cookies

You can clear your browser's local storage/cookies for this site at any time through your browser's settings. Doing so will just mean the cookie notice appears again on your next visit.
