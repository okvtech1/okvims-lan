Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we", "us") built OKV IMS LAN Edition to keep your business data on your own computer, not ours. This policy explains the small amount of information we do collect — when you visit this website or request a trial/activation — and how it's used.

1. What we collect, and when

We only collect information you actively give us:

  • Trial download form (on this website): your name, business name, email, and phone number.
  • Activation request (submitted from inside the software after a purchase): your name, business name, email, phone number, payment reference, and a copy of your payment receipt/screenshot.
  • Direct contact: anything you tell us by email, phone, or WhatsApp when you reach out for support.

We do not collect your business's inventory, sales, customer, or financial data — none of that ever leaves your computer. OKV IMS LAN Edition stores everything locally in a single database file on the server computer you install it on.

2. How we use it

  • To send you the software download link and any related instructions.
  • To review your activation payment and send you an activation code and licence key by email.
  • To respond to support questions you raise with us.
  • To occasionally let you know about updates to the software (you can ask us to stop at any time).

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Where it's stored

Submissions from this website and from the software's activation form are stored in a private Google Sheets & Google Apps Script system that only OKV Technology Consults staff can access. Payment receipt files are stored in a private Google Drive folder, similarly restricted.

4. Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies/local storage needed for the site to function (for example, remembering that you've dismissed the cookie notice). See our Cookie Policy for details.

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it — email technologyokv@gmail.com and we'll action this within a reasonable time.
